Part sampler, part performance instrument, completely fun.

Scratch Baby takes the fundamental behavior of a record being moved by hand and turns it into something you can configure, perform, and make your own. Slow it down, speed it up, scratch back and forth, or glide between pitches—all from your MIDI keyboard.

Under the hood, Scratch Baby gives you precise control over how every key activates the sample and jumps between playback speeds. It has immediacy and playability of a turntable without being confined to its limitations.
